# Incident and postmortem evidence

Use this guide when the target is defensive, corrective, operational, or tied to a regression.

Search the available evidence systems for:

- incident and postmortem records around the introducing or fixing change;
- tickets and review threads linked to the incident;
- team discussion during the affected window;
- dashboards, monitors, logs, traces, and error groups that name the target;
- release and product data showing behavior before and after the change.

Fetch the complete incident record when available. Look for impact, timeline, contributing factors, rejected hypotheses, action items, and follow-up verification. Cross-source identifiers are strong corroboration, but timing correlation alone does not prove motivation.

Return direct rationale separately from circumstantial evidence. Report missing systems and searched-but-empty systems explicitly.
